# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: a production-oriented React and TypeScript single-page application, selected because the repository is empty and the brief requires reusable data-driven routes, responsive interactions, filtering, and future API/CMS readiness. Deployment target is not yet specified.

## Users

People in Kosovo evaluating modern, desirable vehicles and deciding whether to inspect or contact the dealership about a specific car. Their primary jobs are to scan available inventory, understand essential specifications, and reach Auto Merkos without marketplace clutter.

## Product Purpose

Present Auto Merkos and its current vehicles as a focused digital showroom. Success means visitors can quickly understand what is available, inspect a vehicle in depth, and contact the dealership.

## Positioning

Auto Merkos presents a tightly selected inventory through vehicle-first photography and concise information rather than the density, promotion, and comparison mechanics of a used-car marketplace.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse on desktop and mobile, move between a concise homepage, inventory, individual vehicle pages, company information, and contact details. The public interface is entirely in professional Albanian appropriate for Kosovo.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Routes: Ballina, Vetura, a data-driven vehicle detail route, Rreth nesh, and Kontakti.
- Inventory filtering and sorting stay deliberately compact.
- Vehicle records are centralized and shaped for future CMS, database, or API integration.
- The public Instagram profile supplies two phone numbers and the line “Vetura nga KOREA”; the user supplied the Magjistralja Pejë–Deçan location. Other company-history claims, production inventory, and proprietary photography must not be fabricated.
- Demonstration vehicle records and photography must be identified as sample content until replaced.
- The experience must be semantic, keyboard accessible, responsive from 375px through large desktop, reduced-motion aware, and performant with image-heavy content.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: AUTO MERKOS.
- Business: automotive dealership / car sales in Kosovo.
- Voice: concise, direct, serious, and natural Albanian; no inflated luxury clichés or aggressive sales language.
- Brand qualities: quality, trust, modernity, seriousness, confidence.
- The vehicles are the primary content and visual focus.
- Dark, restrained European automotive art direction with near-black, off-white, neutral gray, disciplined typography, and no arbitrary accent color.
- Sherreti is only a quality-level reference; its identity, layout, typography, code, and section structure must not be copied.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with the car, then the model, essential information, and one clear action.
2. Make browsing feel like entering a curated showroom, not searching a marketplace.
3. Earn trust through clarity, restraint, and factual honesty.
4. Keep every public sentence short and useful.
5. Treat mobile composition, image cropping, and touch interaction as first-class design work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain sufficient contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic landmarks and controls, accessible navigation and gallery behavior, comfortable touch targets, and a complete reduced-motion mode.
