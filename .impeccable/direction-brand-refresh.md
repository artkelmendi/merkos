# AUTO MERKOS — Brand-led refresh

## Concept and emotional target

AUTO MERKOS should feel like a precise import marque before it feels like an inventory portal. The memorable thesis is a wide, engineered wordmark moving from **KOREA → KOSOVË**, with a restrained launch sequence that makes the name the first thing visitors remember.

## Typography

- Wordmark: Michroma, tightly tracked and optically compressed to echo the rounded-square, extended character of Microgramma/Eurostile without reproducing the supplied reference.
- Editorial display: Archivo Narrow for large statements and vehicle names.
- Interface/body: Archivo for concise Albanian copy and controls.
- Scale: oversized brand statement, medium editorial section headings, quiet compact metadata.

## Grid and rhythm

- Retain the 1480px maximum canvas and existing 28/72 hero split.
- Follow the hero immediately with a company-first section before inventory.
- Use a broad wordmark, a Korea-to-Kosovo route line, and three factual information columns to create a slower, identity-led pause.
- Mobile becomes a deliberate vertical sequence: identity, statement, route, company facts, then inventory.

## Color and imagery

- Keep the near-black, mineral off-white, and silver system.
- Avoid a new accent color; the Korean flag reference remains textual/emoji only.
- Vehicle photography stays cinematic, while the company section relies on typography, rules, and geographic language so illustrative showroom imagery is not mistaken for documentary photography.

## Motion language

- Signature gesture 1: each AUTO MERKOS letter rises from a clipped baseline on first document load.
- Signature gesture 2: a route line travels from KOREA to KOSOVË as the company section enters.
- Intro duration stays around two seconds, uses only transform/opacity/clip-path, never repeats on client-side route changes, and collapses to an immediate state for reduced-motion users.
- Secondary page reveals remain quieter than the opening identity gesture.

## Interaction and accessibility

- The launch layer is decorative and never focusable.
- Real phone numbers use `tel:` links; Instagram and map links open in a new tab with safe rel attributes.
- All existing keyboard, focus, semantic, and reduced-motion behavior remains intact.
